— 01 / PROCESS How it works

Four steps. You keep the face you walked in with.

STEP 01

Pick where you're going

Choose the country and programme. The exact rules — size, background, file limits — load straight from that authority's own published spec.

STEP 02

Look at the camera

The live coach frames you in real time — tilt, distance, lighting, expression — and grabs the best moment. Or just upload a photo you already have.

STEP 03

We do the boring, legal part

Straighten, balance the light, and clean the background only where the programme permits. Geometry and tone — never your features.

STEP 04

Download, print, go

A plain pass/fix report, then export a single image at true DPI or lay out a print sheet for 4×6, 5×7, Letter or A4.

Every kind of face

Real people. Real rules. Real results.

Glasses, curls, deep skin tones, tricky light — the matte and geometry hold up. These are real pipeline outputs, not mock-ups.

— 02 / HONESTY The honest part

Built to be trusted, not just to pass.

Most photo tools optimise for a green checkmark. We optimise for a photo an officer will actually accept — which means being honest about what the software did and didn't do.

✓ Original quality

Judged on the photo you took

Rules can't be bent from the browser
