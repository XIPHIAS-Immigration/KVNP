FAQ

Common questions.

Will my photo be accepted by Passport Canada or IRCC?Will my photo be accepted by the US State Department or USCIS?

PassportLens checks your photo against the published requirements for the programme you choose — size, head position, background, lighting, expression and file limits — and tells you plainly whether it is OK or what to fix. The final decision always belongs to the issuing authority.

Which countries and documents are supported?

35+ programmes across 26 countries, including the Canadian passport, PR card, citizenship and visitor visa; US passport, visa (DS-160) and diversity visa; UK passport; Indian passport, e-Visa and OCI; Schengen visas; Australia, Japan, Singapore and more.

Can I take the photo with my phone?

Yes. Use the guided camera or upload a photo from your phone. Stand in front of a plain wall in even daylight, and PassportLens will crop, size and clean the background.

Do you change my face?

No. PassportLens never reshapes, retouches or AI-restores a face. It only crops, sizes, cleans the background and evens out lighting. Every applied change is listed in the report.

What do businesses get with Silver?

Unlimited photos for all programmes, one login, a simple CRM to record each client, the photos you made for them, what you charged and whether it's paid, plus receipts, print sheets and email support. Gold and Platinum add staff logins, papers and materials, hardware and setup.

How do I print the photo?

Download a print sheet laid out for 4×6 inch or A4/Letter paper with cut guides, and print it at home, at a pharmacy kiosk or on your studio printer at the DPI the programme requires.
